# Schemes by state.

What exists in each of the 28 states and 8 union territories, and what applies everywhere — counted from the government's own directory, [myScheme.gov.in](https://www.myscheme.gov.in), on **27 September 2026**.

> **A count, not a check.** myScheme is a secondary source — one of its links once pointed at the wrong scheme ([`history/SOURCE_REVIEW_2026-09-09.md`](../history/SOURCE_REVIEW_2026-09-09.md)). Before any scheme here becomes a file, every value must be read at the state department's own page and signed. Nothing below is in the app.

<br>

## The short answer.

| | Schemes |
|---|---|
| **National** — open in every state and UT | **693** |
| **State and UT** schemes, all 36 together | **4154** |
| …of which could help an unorganised worker | **2444** |
| …of which are **pensions** or **worker welfare boards** — the kind the app already handles | **566** |

Every national scheme applies in all 36. That includes the ten already live in the app — nine are listed on myScheme; e-Shram is a registration portal, not a listed scheme.

<br>

## myScheme is not complete.

These are the counts of what myScheme **lists** — a floor, not everything that exists. Searched by state, name and keyword on 27 September 2026, and **not found**:

- **Tamil Nadu's construction-worker and unorganised-worker welfare boards** — which is why Tamil Nadu shows no worker-board schemes below.
- **Karnataka's Sandhya Suraksha** old-age pension.

Both are known from other sources and still to be confirmed at each state's own department. So the deep pass, state by state, has to start from the state's own labour and social-welfare sites — myScheme only gives a head start.

<br>

## How the sorting works.

Each scheme is sorted by keywords in its name, tags, category and description. **Approximate** — spot checks of 40 schemes found the pension and worker-board groups about nine in ten right. "For workers" leaves out scholarships, sports, awards, and honour pensions (freedom fighters, ex-servicemen, journalists).

| Group | Means |
|---|---|
| Pensions | Old-age, widow, disability, destitute and family pensions |
| Worker boards | Construction-worker and labour welfare boards, and schemes for vendors, domestic, migrant, gig and head-load workers |
| Insurance & death | Accident cover, death help, funeral costs |
| Health | Treatment, disability aids |
| Housing · Food & LPG · Women | Houses; ration, gas and meals; marriage and maternity help |
| Livelihood | Loans, self-employment, skills, artisans, fishers |
| Farmers | Agriculture, dairy, livestock |
