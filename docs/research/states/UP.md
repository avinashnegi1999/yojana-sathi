- Row 3: order 3/2016/579/26-2-2016-100(02)/2007, 29 Feb 2016 (online process only): "सक्षम चिकित्साधिकारी के स्तर से निर्गत कम-से-कम 40 प्रतिशत विकलांगता का प्रमाण पत्र संलग्न करना अनिवार्य होगा।" — [PDF](https://sspy-up.gov.in/docs/Important_Go_2021-07-23.pdf)

! **The amount is unresolved.** The official portal says ₹500 a month for rows 1 and 2. Non-official sources say ₹1,000 (₹500 centre + ₹500 state), and one news report's figures agree (₹1,958 crore to 65.28 lakh pensioners in a quarterly instalment ≈ ₹1,000 a month). The portal figure may show one share only, or be stale. **Find the rate order on shasanadesh.up.gov.in before drafting.** Do not use either figure until then.
