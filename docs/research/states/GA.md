## Dayanand Social Security Scheme (DSSS).

| # | Group | ₹ a month | Who qualifies |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Senior citizens | **2,000** | 60+ when applying |
| 2 | Adults with disability | **2,000** | ? disability percentage |
| 3 | Single women and widows under 60 | **2,500** | Woman 18+: widow, divorcee, deserted or judicially separated, or unmarried above 50 |
| 4 | Children with disability | **2,500** | — |
| 5 | Disability 90–100% | **3,500** | continues after 21 |
| 6 | Aids and appliances (disability) | **1,00,000** once | — |

All: "bonafide resident of the state of Goa by birth or by domicile of 15 years"; "Income Limit is Rs. 24000/- P.A".

> "Single Woman: Woman who is above the age of 18 years and includes those whose husband has expired, divorcees, deserted or judicially separated woman and unmarried woman above the age of 50."
>
> "Financial assistance of Rs.1,00,000/- is provided to persons with Disabilities category for purchase of aids & appliances."

! **Check the income limit.** ₹24,000 a year is far below other states' limits; confirm whether it is per year or a typo.
- Documents: age proof, **15-year residence certificate**, income certificate or ₹50 stamp-paper self-declaration, marriage certificate (single-woman self-declaration if unmarried).
