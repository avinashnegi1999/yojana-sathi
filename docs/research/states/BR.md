- Scheme list: the six "Progress Report" names on the official portal.

! **Amount unresolved.** Several non-official sources report that from July 2025 all these pensions pay **₹1,100 a month**, paid on the 10th, with e-KYC required. Find the 2025 order before drafting; the 2019 figures are superseded if so.

! Row 1 has **no income test** — unusual, and it fits the app with no new question (age + "no other pension").
