# Nagaland — schemes, from official pages.

Research notes, **not signed**. Read on **27 September 2026**.

Source: Department of Social Welfare — <https://dsw.nagaland.gov.in/about-the-national-social-assistance-programme-nsap/> (the page cites 2014–15 figures).

| # | Scheme | ₹ a month | Who qualifies |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | IGNOAPS | 200 (60–79) · 500 (80+) | 60+, below poverty line |
| 2 | IGNWPS | 300 · 500 from 80 | Widow 40+, below poverty line |
| 3 | IGNDPS | 300 · 500 from 80 | 18+, disability above 80%, below poverty line |
| 4 | NFBS | 20,000 once | Death of the breadwinner aged 18–59 |

> "Amount: – The pension amount is Rs. 200 per month for individuals aged between 60-79 and Rs. 500 for persons who are 80 years and above."

! These are the **central rates only**, and the page is old (2014–15 beneficiary counts). Whether Nagaland adds a state top-up now is not stated — confirm before drafting.
