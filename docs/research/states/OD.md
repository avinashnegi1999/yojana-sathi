## Madhu Babu Pension Yojana (MBPY) and NSAP.

| # | Who | ₹ a month | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | MBPY and NSAP beneficiaries **aged 80+** | **3,500** from January 2025 | Notification 9 Jan 2025 (below) |
| 2 | MBPY and NSAP beneficiaries with **80%+ disability** | **3,500** from January 2025 | same |
| 3 | Other MBPY beneficiaries (old age, widow, disabled, …) | ? base rate not yet found in the bundle | — |

> "No. PT1-SSEPD-SS2-SCH-0003-2024/371, In pursuance of rule (7) of Madhu Babu Pension Yojana Rules, 2008, the State Government is pleased to enhance the social security pension of beneficiaries aged 80 years and above and beneficiaries with not less than 80% disability to Rs. 3,500/- per month with effect from January, 2025. This notification supersedes all previous notifications issued in this regard." — Bhubaneswar, 9 January 2025, R.S. Gopalan, Commissioner-cum-Secretary.

- The department's dashboard confirms the category: "Beneficiaries Aged 80+ or 80%+ Disability Under MBPY & NSAP".
- MBPY Rules, 2008 (WCD Resolution No. 11-I-SD-50/2007-WCD, 4 Jan 2008) set eligibility in **Rule 6**; amendments add categories: orphan children of COVID-affected families (Res. 4051, 27.05.2021, no income ceiling), COVID widows (letter 4182, 04.06.2021), MISA detainees (Res. 2864, 12.04.2023).
- ? Rule 6's base categories and income limits are further in the bundle — read before drafting.
