## NTR Bharosa Pension Scheme.

**Amount:** raised from ₹3,000 to **₹4,000 a month from April 2024** (portal banner, Telugu):
> "రూ.3వేల నుంచి రూ.4 వేలకు పెంపు - ఏప్రిల్‌ 2024 నుంచి అమలు"

? The ₹4,000 applies to the general categories; disability and other categories are reported (non-official) at higher rates — confirm per category.

**Categories** (memo, Annexure I, section 6(b)):

| # | Category | Sanctioned through |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Old age | CEO, SERP |
| 2 | Widow | CEO, SERP |
| 3 | Single women | CEO, SERP (via Tahsildar) |
| 4 | Disabled | CEO, SERP |
| 5 | Weavers | Handlooms & Textiles |
| 6 | Fishermen | Fisheries |
| 7 | Dappu artists | Social Welfare |
| 8 | Traditional cobblers | Social Welfare |
| 9 | Transgender | Health & Family Welfare |
| 10 | Toddy tappers | Prohibition & Excise |

- **Eligibility** follows "the common eligibility criteria prescribed under the NTR Bharosa Pension Scheme guidelines issued vide G.O.Ms.No.174, PR&RD (RD.I) Dept dated 13.12.2019" — ? not yet read.
- **Apply** at the Swarna Grama / Swarna Wardu office with documents; eKYC; physical verification; MPDO / Municipal Commissioner recommends; SERP sanctions.
- ! **Application window:** new applications were taken **07.09.2026 – 16.09.2026** (timeline table). It is closed now; future windows depend on government orders.
- ! **Not backdated:** "The pension shall be sanctioned and released only from the month in which SERP issues the sanction … and shall not be payable retrospectively".
