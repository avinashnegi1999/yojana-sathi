> "The Directorate maintains the following social security and pension schemes: Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS) Widow Pension Scheme Disabled Pension Sandhya Suraksha Scheme"

- Non-official sources: Karnataka runs **nine** monthly pensions (old age, disability, widow, unmarried / divorced women "Manaswini", transgender "Mythri", acid-attack victims, endosulfan-affected, and others); Sandhya Suraksha is 65+ with spouse income ≤ ₹20,000 a year; amounts reported between ₹500 and ₹2,000 — **all unconfirmed**.
- ! myScheme also lacks Sandhya Suraksha (checked earlier). No official page with amounts was reachable on 27 Sep 2026.
