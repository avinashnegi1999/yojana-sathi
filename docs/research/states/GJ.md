## Pensions.

| # | Scheme | ₹ a month | Who qualifies |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ganga Swarupa Arthik Sahay (widow pension) | **1,250** (since April 2019) | Widow; **age rule conflicts** (below); income limit reported ₹1.2 lakh rural / ₹1.5 lakh urban (non-official) |
| 2 | Old age (Indira Gandhi Vruddh Sahay / Niradhar Vruddh Sahay) | ? | ? |
| 3 | Disability pension | ? | ? |

> "The state govt's earlier "Vidhva Sahay Yojna" was renamed as "Ganga Swarupa Yojna" to lend respectability to the beneficiaries. The Gujarat govt. had raised the monthly pension amount from Rs. 1,000 to Rs. 1,250 since April 2019."
>
> "Every month a pension of Rs 1250 will be provided to all the beneficiary women."
>
> "For this scheme, your age must be between 18 years to 40 years."

! **Age conflict.** The district page says 18–40; non-official sources say 18+ or 18–50. Read the WCD department's own rule before drafting.

- Apply: rural — Village Computer Entrepreneur (VCE); urban — Mamlatdar office (district page).
