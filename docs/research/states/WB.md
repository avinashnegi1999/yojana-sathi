## Jai Bangla state pensions and Lakshmir Bhandar.

Jai Bangla groups the state pensions (old age, widow, disability, Taposili Bandhu for SC, Joy Johar for ST, farmers, fishermen, artisans, weavers and others). Lakshmir Bhandar pays women a monthly amount and moves them into Jai Bangla at 60. **No official page could be read on 27 Sep 2026** — amounts and rules stay unrecorded.

<br>

## Construction and unorganised workers.

West Bengal runs the Bina Mulye Samajik Suraksha Yojana (BMSSY) and a Migrant Workers' Welfare Scheme through the labour department. The official portal is staff-only. myScheme lists 15 West Bengal worker-board schemes (e.g. migrant workers' accident, cremation and death help) — secondary, to confirm.
