## Integrated Social Security Scheme (ISSS).

A UT-run scheme, "funded on State Plan/Non-Plan on 50:50 basis", with four components:

| # | Component | ₹ a month | Who qualifies |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Old Age Pension (OAP) | **1,000** per the district page — see below | **Men 60+, women 55+** |
| 2 | Widows / Women in Distress (WID) | 1,000 (same) | Widow / divorcee **40+** |
| 3 | Pension for Physically Challenged Persons (PCP) | 1,000 (same) | Disability **40%+** |
| 4 | Pension for Transgender Community (TG) | ? | ? |

> "a monthly pension of Rs. 1000/- is provided to men above the age of 60 years, women above the age of 55 years, widow/Divorcee above 40 years of age & physically Challenged persons with disability of 40% or above." (District Doda)

! **Possibly superseded.** Non-official sources report enhanced rates from **April 2025**: under 60 ₹1,250; 60–79 ₹1,500; 80+ ₹2,000. Not on these pages — find the order.
