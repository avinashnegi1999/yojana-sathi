## Financial assistance schemes.

| # | Scheme | ₹ a month | Who qualifies |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Financial Assistance to Senior Citizens (old age) | **2,000** (60–69), **+500** for SC / ST / minority; **2,500** (70+) | 60+; **5 years' residence in Delhi**; **annual family income < ₹1,00,000** (applicant + spouse, all sources); single-operated Aadhaar-mapped bank account in Delhi |
| 2 | Financial assistance, ages 0–59 (disability, per the page order) | **2,500** | 0–59; 5 years' residence; Aadhaar-mapped account (relaxed for minors) — ? remaining criteria on the page |

> "To persons above 60 years · Residence of min. 5 years in Delhi before the date of application. · Annual family income of less than Rs 1,00,000 from all sources (including rent, interest/dividends on savings & investments, earnings from farm, property sale proceeds etc.) of the applicant & spouse."
>
> "Rs 2000 p.m. for 60-69 years, with additional Rs. 500 p.m. to SC/ST/Minority community beneficiaries · Rs 2500 p.m. for +70 beneficiaries"
>
> "To persons between 0- 59 years … Rs. 2500/- per month."

- SC / ST applicants 60–69 submit a caste certificate; minority applicants a verified self-declaration of religion.
- ? Row 2's scheme name and other criteria (disability percentage) — confirm on the page; the women-in-distress (widow) scheme is also on this department's list.
