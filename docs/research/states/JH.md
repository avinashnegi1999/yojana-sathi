# Jharkhand — schemes, from official pages.

Research notes, **not signed**. Read on **27 September 2026**.

Source: Jharkhand Mukhyamantri Maiyan Samman Yojana — <https://mmmsy.jharkhand.gov.in/> (Women & Child Development Department; amount read from the page as rendered in a browser).

<br>

## Mukhyamantri Maiyan Samman Yojana.

| # | Scheme | ₹ a month | Who qualifies |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Jharkhand Mukhyamantri Maiyan Samman Yojana | **2,500** per beneficiary, paid by the **15th** of every month | Woman resident of Jharkhand, **18 to under 50** when applying; Aadhaar-linked single bank account; family holds an Antyodaya (yellow), Priority Household (pink), K-Oil (white) or green ration card |

> "आवेदन के समय महिला 18 वर्ष की आयु पूर्ण कर चुकी हो तथा 50 वर्ष से कम आयु की हो।"
>
> "आवेदिका का परिवार झारखण्ड राज्य के अंत्योदय अन्न योजना कार्ड (पीला रंग का राशन कार्ड) धारी हो अथवा पूर्वविक्ता प्राप्त गृहस्थ कार्ड (गुलाबी रंग का राशन कार्ड) धारी हो अथवा K-Oil राशन कार्ड (सफेद राशन कार्ड) धारी हो अथवा हरा रंग का पृथक राशन कार्ड धारी हो।"
>
> Page counter: "15 तारीख तक हर महीने संवितरण · 2500 की सम्मान राशि प्रति माह | प्रति लाभार्थी"

**Not eligible** if the applicant — or her husband, or her father if she is unmarried —
- is a regular, permanent, contract or honorarium employee of the central / state government, a PSU, a statutory, local or urban body, or a government-aided school, or draws a pension / family pension after retirement;
- has a family member who is a current or former MP / MLA;
- is in a family paying income tax (spouse, unmarried and disabled children);
- is in EPF (the list continues on the page).
