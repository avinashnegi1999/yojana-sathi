Widow Pension Scheme
Apply Online :
https://ssp.uk.gov.in/OnlineRegistration/FrmWidowOnlineApplicationForm.aspx
Eligibility for Widow Pension Scheme
(i) The age of the applicant should not be less than 18 years.
(ii) Monthly income should not be more than Rs. 4000/- or the applicant should be a BPL card holder.
(iii) The applicant should have been selected in an open meeting of the Gram Sabha.
(iv) Husband’s death certificate.
Documents required for online application
(i) Income certificate of Rs. 4000/- per month issued online from all sources or photocopy of BPL card.
(ii) Copy of family register issued by VPDO(Village Panchayat Development Officer) for applicants from rural areas or photocopy of ration card for applicants from urban areas.
(iii) Copy of the death certificate of the applicant’s husband.
(iv) Copy of the proposal selected in the open meeting of Gram Panchayat/Councilor.
(v) Certified photo of the applicant (from Pradhan/VPDO/Sabhashad/Councillor).
(vi) Photocopy of CBS bank account passbook.
(vii) Photocopy of Aadhar card.
