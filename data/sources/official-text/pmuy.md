FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ON UJJWALA Scheme
Q1 Who is the eligible beneficiary under the PMUY scheme?
Ans :- Adult woman from poor household Based on submission of a Deprivation declaration (as per standard format).
To be eligible for a PMUY connection, the household must not have an existing LPG connection registered in the name of any family member listed in the family composition document.
Q2 What is the criterion to ascertain a Poor household under PMUY Scheme?
Ans :- The deprivation declaration submitted by the applicant is the basic criteria to consider it as an eligible Poor household under the scheme.
Q3 What are the benefits in PMUY?
Ans :- A PMUY beneficiary receives Security deposit free LPG Connection with no installation charges which covers:
14.2 Kg LPG Cylinder/5 Kg LPG Cylinder(s)
Domestic Pressure Regulator
1.2 m Surakhsa Hose pipe
DGCC booklet
Free first refill(s)
Free two burner stove
A PMUY beneficiary does not need to pay any amount to the distributor for any of the above.
Q4 How can one apply for LPG connection under the scheme?
Ans :- Applicants can apply via following methods:
Online – Customers can apply online through the application form available at https://pmuy.gov.in or visit the nearest CSC Centre for assistance in submitting the application. There is no fee for self-submission. However, a charge of ₹20 will be applicable if the application is submitted through CSC Centres.
Offline – Customers can directly submit the application at preferably nearest LPG distributorship of OMCs.
Q5 Is there any fee at the time of getting a PMUY connection?
Ans :-
5(a). No. The customer does not have to pay anything while taking a new connection.
(If any stamp fee is applicable for the state, same has to be paid by the beneficiary)
5(b). Can customers avail LPG Stove and First refill, free of Cost?
Yes, Under PMUY scheme, OMCs will be providing LPG stove and First Refill at free of Cost to the customer. Hence, customers need not pay anything while taking LPG connection under the scheme.
Q6 Which documents are mandatory to be submitted by an applicant for enrolment under PMUY Scheme?
Ans :- The applicant must provide following documents:
Application/KYC form in the standard format, affixed with the applicant's recent photograph and bearing their signature.
Copy of Aadhaar card of Applicant as Proof of identity (PoI)
Copy of Aadhaar card of all adult family members as mentioned in Ration Card or other family composition document.
Proof of Address (POA) – Aadhaar card can be used as PoA as well, if the current address on which the applicant requires connection is same as the address on Aadhaar. If the address is different from the Aadhaar card then applicant can submit one of the documents as per approved list of POA (as available in the KYC/application form containing their current address details
Bank account details of the applicant along with a copy of the passbook /cancelled cheque. This is required for transfer of LPG subsidy to beneficiaries account through DBTL.
Copy of Latest Ration card. In states, where the State Government portal has updated family details, a self signed copy of the printout from this portal can also be submitted by the beneficiary in lieu of ration card.
If the states where Ration card does not define the family composition or Ration card is not available, any other document issued by Central/State Govt. certifying family composition of the identified household in which applicant's name appears, to be submitted.
Deprivation declaration in support of Poor household duly signed by applicant as per standard format.
For Migrant applicants- Self declaration as per standard format for PoA and family composition.
Q7 Can I opt for a 5 Kg connection under the scheme?
Ans :- Applicant can choose between 14.2 kg single bottle cylinder, 5 kg Single bottle Cylinder or 5 Kg Double bottle cylinder connection.
Q8 What document can be produced as Proof of Identity (POI) and Proof of Address (POA)?
Ans :- Please refer to reply to Question 6.
Q9 Under the PMUY scheme, is it mandatory to collect Aadhaar for all adult family members as per the age in ration card?
Ans :- Yes, the adult family members are to be ascertained based on the age as on date of KYC, and Aadhaar details of all adult family members to be collected from the applicant.
Q10 What should I do if my Aadhaar or family members' Aadhaar is not updated or contains errors or is inactive?
Ans :- You must get your Aadhaar details corrected/updated at the nearest Aadhaar Seva Kendra or online through UIDAI website ( https://uidai.gov.in/en/my-aadhaar/update-aadhaar.html ) / mAadhaar app before applying, as correct Aadhaar details are mandatory for PMUY application.
Q11 What type of Ration Card needs to be submitted by the applicant?
Ans :- As Ration Card is only for the purpose of identifying the family Composition of the beneficiary, any type of digitized Ration card/family composition document as issued by the respective state to be collected from the applicant.
Q12 Can PMUY Connection be released to Poor Household with no female adult members?
Ans :- No. PMUY connections can only be released in the name of adult female members of the Poor household.
Q13 Can a male member apply for a connection under PMUY?
Ans :- No.
Q14 Whether a connection can be given to a female who is minor and claiming to be an only female member in the household?
Ans :- No.
Q15 Whether a connection can be given to a male member in the household if there are no female members or the female member is a minor?
Ans :- No.
Q16 Whether a connection can be given to a deceased female member in the household if there are only male members now and her name is still appearing in the Ration card or family composition document?
Ans :- No.
Q17 Is Biometric Aadhaar authentication (eKYC) mandatory for connections enrolled under the PMUY scheme? If so, how is it carried out?
Ans :- Yes. Biometric Aadhaar authentication is mandatory and it will be carried out by the LPG distributor of OMCs.
Q18 An SC /ST applicant who is unable to submit her caste certificate and wants a connection under the scheme. Can it be enrolled? If so, will the connection be tagged under SC/ST?
Ans :- Yes, the applicant can be given a connection provided she fulfils other eligibility criteria as per scheme guidelines. Provision will be available in the KYC to select SC or ST category for the applicant without any requirement of caste certificate.
Q19 Can the earlier submitted applications be considered under the scheme?
Ans :- Yes. Applications will be considered under the scheme only if the applicant meets the eligibility criteria and submits the required documents (Revised Application/KYC form along with Deprivation declaration and other supporting documents) as per the latest guidelines.
Q20 The KYCs were seeded in 2024 or earlier and at that time, one member of the family was minor but has become major now. Does this member need to be added in KYC for connection under the scheme?
Ans :- Yes. The member is required to be declared in the KYC and will be required to submit their Aadhaar as well along with other required documents (Revised Application/KYC form along with deprivation declaration and other supporting documents).
Q21 The KYCs were seeded in 2024 or earlier and now the Ration Card number of the beneficiary has changed. How will this consumer be enrolled under the scheme?
Ans :- Yes. Aadhaar of all adult family members as per revised document to be produced by the applicant along with other required documents (Revised Application/KYC form along with deprivation declaration and other supporting documents)
Q22 The KYCs were seeded in 2024 or earlier and the Bank account or mobile number of the beneficiary seeded earlier has changed (due to merger or other issue). How will the same be updated?
Ans :- Yes, it can be changed. Applicant is required to produce revised document/details along with other required documents (Revised Application/KYC form along with deprivation declaration and other supporting documents)
Q23 My family has a PMUY connection at home in the name of my mother-in-law and she moves with me and my family to the city where we work. Can a new PMUY connection be taken in the name of my mother – in -law?
Ans :- No. Since the applicant (Mother-in-law) is already having a connection, another connection cannot be released in her name, but the connection can be transferred to the city.
Q24 Can I transfer my PMUY connection to another address or city? What is the process?
Ans :- Yes. PMUY Customer need to submit the request letter along with proof of identity to the attached distributor.
Q25 I was living in One state (eg. Uttar Pradesh) and am now migrating to another state (eg. Madhya Pradesh) with my entire family. I have a PMUY connection. What is the process to transfer the connection to the new location?
Ans :- PMUY Customer need to submit the request letter along with proof of identity to the attached distributor.
Q26 A Family already holding PMUY Connection is split into two and resides at different addresses. Ration cards have been taken for both these families with suitable amendment in family composition, however Aadhaar of all adult members of this split family are seeded in the existing connection. Whether the new split family can avail a PMUY Connection?
Ans :- In case the family is split and Aadhaar of family members are linked with previous connection, the applicant needs to contact the concerned distributor / OMC to delete/inactivate the family members moved out of the household having LPG connection by submitting a new ration card. After which, the applicant can submit all the documents mentioned in reply to Q (6).
Q27 Is Pre- Installation inspection mandatory.
Ans :- Yes. Prior to release of a PMUY connection, pre-installation Inspection will be carried out at Applicant's Premise as per standard format.
Q28 The ration card issued by the State, which does not have the complete list of family members. Female RC holders have their father's name on the RC even after marriage. How is the family composition to be established in such cases for Aadhaar seeding of family members?
Ans :- In in such case where family composition is not defined under Ration Card, the applicant needs to submit any other document issued by Central/State Govt. certifying family composition which contains the complete family composition details of the applicant.
Q29 Are there any categories like SC/ST/MBC/Forest Dwellers under the current scheme?
Ans :- No. However, in the KYC/Application form, an option is available for the applicant to select whether she belongs to General or SC or ST or other caste category.
Q30 If I do not have a Ration Card, what other document can I provide as family composition proof?
Ans :- You may submit a valid state/central government-issued family composition document showing names and ages of all family members.Additionally you will be required to submit Aadhaar number and Copy of Aadhaar card of all adult family members as mentioned in the family composition document.
Q31 I have a PNG connection. I heard that I can take an LPG connection as well. Can I get a PMUY connection?
Ans :- PNG connection holders are only allowed to take Non subsidized LPG connection. So, You can not get a PMUY connection as well as a Non PMUY subsidized connection.
Q32 I am a PMUY connection holder. I am taking a PNG connection. Can I sell / give my Cylinder and stove to my neighbor?
Ans :- The PMUY cylinder and stove are subsidized assets and cannot be sold or transferred to anyone else. You need to surrender the asset back to the Oil marketing companies and request for termination of the PMUY LPG connection through your distributor.
Q33 I have submitted my PMUY application but haven't received any update. What should I do?
Ans :- The status of connection can be obtained from the distributor or by contacting toll free number – 1800 266 6696.
Q34 I received my PMUY connection a few months back, but I am not getting the subsidy for my LPG refills (or) I have been using my PMUY connection for the past two years and was receiving subsidy earlier. However, I haven't received subsidy for the last few refills. What steps should I take?
Ans :- Beneficiaries are requested to visit their bank to verify the following:
Whether their Aadhaar is seeded with their bank account, and
Whether their Aadhaar is correctly mapped in the NPCI mapper with the correct and active bank account.
If Aadhaar is not seeded or mapped correctly, the consumer can fill out the "Aadhaar Seeding Process" form and submit it at the bank.
Beneficiaries can check the Aadhaar mapping status online by visiting the NPCI website: https://www.npci.org.in
Navigation path: Consumer Tab → Bharat Aadhaar Seeding Enabler (BASE) → Aadhaar Mapped Status
The status should display "Enabled for DBT".
If not, consumers can map their Aadhaar online via the NPCI website by navigating to: Consumer Tab → Bharat Aadhaar Seeding Enabler (BASE) → Aadhaar Seeding/Deseeding
Alternatively, consumer can:
Contact their respective LPG distributorship,
Call the general helpline number: 1800 2333 555, or 1800 266 6696
Raise a grievance through the respective OMC websites, consumer portals, or mobile applications.
